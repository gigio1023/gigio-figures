# Figure style provenance

The design system was rebuilt in 2026-09 after a figure set produced with the previous editorial style read as crude to its domain readers. A diagnosis of that set found the causes in the style rules rather than the tools: one accent fill carried more than ten meanings across the document, every node had the same box and stroke, emphasis by stroke weight was forbidden, concept names were set in monospace, zoom insets were banned as decoration, and nothing planned the figures as a set.

The replacement draws on four kinds of evidence, all inspected directly in 2026-09:

- **Published figures.** Figures from Anthropic and Transformer Circuits, OpenAI, Google DeepMind and Google Research, Meta, Apple, Microsoft Research, Hugging Face, DeepSeek, Qwen, Moonshot, MiniMax, Zhipu, ByteDance Seed, Xiaomi MiMo, and independent explainers were downloaded or extracted from their PDFs, viewed, and measured (fonts with `pdffonts`, palettes and colored-area fractions with pixel sampling, label size relative to figure width). The rules that recur across the strongest figures became the grammar: an emphasis budget with ghosted context, one meaning per hue, delta highlights and sibling variants for comparisons, shapes that mean kinds, and baked opaque backgrounds. `skills/technical-figure/references/exemplars.md` lists the figures and lessons. No artwork is redistributed.
- **Perception and diagram research.** Bertin, Mackinlay, Cleveland and McGill, Munzner, Tufte, Larkin and Simon, Tversky, Moody's Physics of Notations, Gestalt grouping studies, Mayer's multimedia principles, Gleicher's comparison taxonomy, Purchase's layout aesthetics, and design-system guidance (IBM, Azure, AWS, C4, Nature). Sources are listed in `skills/technical-figure/references/grammar.md`.
- **Measurements of the delivery surface.** Notion's CSS color tokens in light and dark themes, its column width, and 2x renders of candidate font stacks and stroke widths at that width. The theme policy follows from the finding that no single ink color reaches 4.5:1 text contrast on both backgrounds, and from published transparent PNGs that lose arrows on dark pages.
- **Figure-generation research and tool trials.** Studies of LLM and VLM figure generation (FigTree, PaperBanana, AutoFigure, DiagramAgent, and judge-reliability benchmarks) and a trial that drew the same mechanism figure through d2, Mermaid, Graphviz, Typst, and elkjs with a custom SVG renderer. They set the pipeline: a written spec, a program-rendered SVG with measured text, deterministic lint before visual review, item-by-item checks against the spec, pairwise comparison instead of scores, and an accumulating defect list.

Typefaces are Pretendard and JetBrains Mono (both SIL OFL 1.1) with MathJax for math. Brand typefaces and brand assets of any publisher are not used, and figures made with this system must not claim affiliation with any publisher whose work informed it.

The canonical runtime tokens live in `shared/figure-style/tokens.json`.

## Capability audit, 2026-10

The earlier rule set mixed content invariants with production defaults: it allowed meaningful quantities in the entry point but prohibited all counts and qualifiers in grammar and review, capped revisions at three, required one tool route, and instructed ordinary figure work to accumulate skill corrections. The maintenance revision keeps exact relationships, math, measurements, real-font geometry checks, accessible encodings, light defaults, dark legibility, and rendered review. It makes tool and style choices conditional on the artifact, applies one content rule across the three skills, replaces the revision cap with progress and budget decisions, and requires existing project authority or explicit skill maintenance for persistent corrections. These are instruction changes; they do not establish a measured gain in model performance.
